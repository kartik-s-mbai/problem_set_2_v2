## 2. My findings

### Finding 1

- Where: https://firsttimehdb.vercel.app/, main heading and resale price results.
- What I did, what I saw: I opened the site and looked at the resale prices for different towns and flat types. The site says it helps determine whether a flat fits the user's housing budget, but it does not ask about salary, CPF, grants, loan eligibility, interest rates, or other personal financial information. What it actually does is show resale price benchmarks using real HDB transaction data.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The current data already supports a resale price benchmark, so the wording can be changed without changing the API or calculations.
- Severity, and why: 3. The wording can give the user the wrong expectation about what the product actually tells them, especially when they are making a major financial decision.
- The repair: Make it clear that the product shows HDB resale price benchmarks and does not calculate personal affordability.

### Finding 2

- Where: https://firsttimehdb.vercel.app/, Town dropdown and the 3 Room, 4 Room, and 5 Room result cards.
- What I did, what I saw: I selected a town and could see the three flat types for that town. When I wanted to compare another town, I had to change the dropdown and replace the first set of results. I could not see places like Tampines, Bedok, and Pasir Ris together.
- Which heuristic: 7, Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: System. The page currently keeps results for one selected town at a time, so proper town comparison would require loading and keeping results for more than one town.
- Severity, and why: 2. The site still works, but someone comparing several areas has to repeat the same process over and over.
- The repair: Give users a way to compare two or more towns without replacing the results they were already looking at.

### Finding 3

- Where: https://firsttimehdb.vercel.app/, the resale price cards.
- What I did, what I saw: I looked at the median resale price, price range, price per square foot, remaining lease, month, and transaction count. The numbers are shown clearly, but the page does not explain how the median and other figures are calculated or exactly which transactions are included.
- Which heuristic: 10, Help and Documentation.
- Screen or system: Screen. The calculations already exist in the system, so the page only needs a short explanation of what the figures mean.
- Severity, and why: 2. The user can still use the site, but these are large financial figures and it should be clear what they are based on.
- The repair: Add a short explanation or tooltip saying how the median, range, price per square foot, remaining lease, and transaction count are calculated.

### Finding 4

- Where: https://firsttimehdb.vercel.app/, the 3 Room, 4 Room, and 5 Room result cards.
- What I did, what I saw: I looked at each card and saw the median price, range, price per square foot, remaining lease, transaction count, and month together. The median price stands out clearly, but there is still a lot of supporting information inside each card for someone who only wants a quick price comparison.
- Which heuristic: 8, Aesthetic and Minimalist Design.
- Screen or system: Screen. The same information can be reorganized without changing the underlying data.
- Severity, and why: 1. It does not stop anyone from using the site, but some of the supporting information could distract from the main price.
- The repair: Keep the median resale price as the main focus and make the supporting figures less prominent or place them under a small details section.
