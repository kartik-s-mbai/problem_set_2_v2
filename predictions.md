## Predictions

Before looking at the feedback, I think the product is pretty simple and easy to use overall. I do not think the main problems will come from the layout or basic navigation.

I think most of the issues will come from what the results actually mean, how clearly that is explained, and how useful the tool is when someone wants to compare different HDB options.

### Visibility of System Status

I think the product could be clearer about the data being shown.

The user gets resale price information, but it is not immediately obvious how recent the data is, how many transactions were used, or what time period the results are based on.

I think someone will probably point out that the product should show things like the date range of the data or the number of transactions used.

### Match Between the System and the Real World

I think this could be one of the bigger issues.

The product is called an HDB Resale Budget Checker, but it does not actually calculate someone's personal budget.

It does not ask about salary, CPF, grants, loan eligibility, interest rates, or anything else related to financing. What it actually does is use real HDB resale data to show what flats have been selling for.

Because of that, I think someone may say that the name "Budget Checker" makes the product sound like it does more than it actually does.

The name may need to change, or the product needs to make it clearer that it is mainly checking resale prices rather than telling someone what they can afford.

### User Control and Freedom

I think the product is easy enough to use, but comparing multiple towns could get annoying.

For example, if someone wants to compare Tampines, Bedok, and Pasir Ris, they have to keep changing the selection instead of being able to see them together.

I think this could lead to feedback about adding a comparison feature or making it easier to move between different towns.

### Consistency and Standards

I do not think this will be a major problem.

The product uses normal HDB terms like 3 room, 4 room, and 5 room, so there is not much for the user to figure out.

The result cards also follow the same format, which makes the information easy to understand once the user has seen one of them.

If anything comes up here, I think it will be something small.

### Error Prevention

I think this is one of the stronger parts of the product.

Users pick towns and flat types from the options provided instead of typing everything manually. That reduces the chance of spelling mistakes or entering something the system does not recognize.

I do not expect much criticism here.

### Recognition Rather Than Recall

I also think this will be a strength.

The user does not have to remember exact HDB town names or flat type labels because the options are already there.

They can just pick what they want instead of having to remember and type it.

I do not expect this to be a major issue.

### Flexibility and Efficiency of Use

I think the product works well if someone just wants to check one town.

The weakness comes when someone wants to compare several places.

A first time buyer could easily be deciding between Tampines, Bedok, Pasir Ris, and other towns at the same time. Right now, they would have to keep switching between them.

I think this could lead to feedback about letting users compare multiple towns or flat types on the same screen.

### Aesthetic and Minimalist Design

I think this will mostly be seen as a strength.

The product does not throw a huge amount of information at the user, and most of what is there is directly related to HDB resale prices.

I do not think people will see the interface as cluttered.

If there is criticism here, it will probably be about whether everything shown inside the result cards is actually necessary.

### Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover From Errors

I think this could become a problem if the user gets no results or if something goes wrong with the API.

The user should be able to tell the difference between there being no matching transactions and the system failing to load the data.

If the message is too generic, they will not know what happened or what they should do next.

I think the feedback may be that the error messages need to be clearer and actually tell the user what they can do next.

### Help and Documentation

I think this could be another bigger issue.

The product gives users price information, but it could do a better job of explaining where those numbers come from.

Someone may want to know what data is being used, what time period it covers, how the price was calculated, and how many transactions were included.

If the product gives someone a number like $600,000, the obvious question is: $600,000 based on what?

I think the product may need a short explanation of the data source and how the numbers are calculated.

## Overall Prediction

Overall, I think the product will be seen as simple and easy to use.

I do not think the biggest problems will be the layout, navigation, or basic HDB terminology.

I think most of the useful criticism will come down to:

- "Budget Checker" may make the product sound like it calculates personal affordability when it does not.
- The product could explain its price data more clearly.
- It works well for checking one town, but comparing several towns takes more effort than it should.

The prediction I am least sure about is the error messages because someone would probably have to create a situation where no results appear or something fails.

These are my predictions before looking at any of the feedback.
